Nature & Landscape

Light, land & open air.

Coastlines, gardens and golden hour — photography that lets the outdoors do the talking.

Sessions built around natural light

Nature photography here means working with what Lagos already gives us — beach light at Elegushi and Alpha, the green of Lekki Conservation Centre, quiet gardens at golden hour. No studio walls, just patient framing and timing.

Good for solo portraits set in landscape, couples, small groups, or pure landscape work for personal or commercial use — prints, wallpapers, brand backdrops.

Sessions are scheduled around sunrise or the last two hours before sunset, when the light is softest and most flattering.
